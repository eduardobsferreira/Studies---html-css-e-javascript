Discover Brazil: A Package for Every Kind of Traveler Brazil is a country of extraordinary contrasts — golden beaches, dense rainforest, thundering waterfalls, colonial towns, and cities that pulse with music and energy. Whatever kind of trip you're dreaming of, there's a corner of Brazil built for it, and a package designed to get you there without the guesswork.

Packages

Explore Our Travel Packages Rio de Janeiro & the Coast — Sugarloaf Mountain, Christ the Redeemer, Copacabana, and Ipanema, with sunsets from Arpoador. Iconic landmarks meet laid-back beach life. The Amazon Rainforest — Guided jungle treks, river cruises, and eco-lodge stays deep in the canopy. Wildlife spotting and encounters with local communities in the world's largest rainforest. Iguazu Falls — Both the Brazilian and Argentine sides of the falls, boat rides through the spray, and time in the surrounding national park. Pantanal Wildlife Safari — Jaguars, caimans, capybaras, and hundreds of bird species in the world's largest tropical wetland. Ideal for adventure off the beaten path. Salvador & the Northeast Beaches — Afro-Brazilian culture, colonial architecture, and some of Brazil's most stunning coastline in Bahia and beyond. Carnival & Cultural Experiences — Itineraries built around Brazil's world-famous festivals, local cuisine, samba, and generations-old craft traditions.
